# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination of any kind are not
tolerated. Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub;
they will be handled confidentially and promptly.

This is a short form of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, which applies in full.
