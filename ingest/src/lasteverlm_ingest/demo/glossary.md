# A short glossary of Zeltavian words and customs

Compiled for visitors to Zeltavia, a small fictional country on the northern sea whose capital, Marrow Bay, lies where the river Quill meets the coast.

**brennet** — A greeting meaning "you are expected". It is said at any hour, to strangers and to returning fishermen alike; answering it with "brennet" back means you intend to stay for tea.

**quillwater tea** — A pale tea brewed from river mint and dried juniper. Refusing the first cup is rude; the second cup may be quietly poured into a plant, and most Zeltavian houses keep a well-watered plant by the door for that purpose.

**the Knelling** — The September juniper harvest on the Juniper Steps, north of Quill Ford. The first basket of berries is offered to the river Quill and the second to whoever sings worst.

**smokra** — Smoked eel with juniper, the national dish. Smokra is cooked in the smokehouses along the Eel Steps in Marrow Bay; a proper smokra is said to be ready "when the gulls start to gather".

**lantern duty** — The custom of lighting the signal lantern on Lantern Hill each night. The duty falls to the family that lost an argument in the town council that week, so a lantern lit late is a sign of a close vote.

**pell** — A count that is generous on purpose, after the Pell family of harbourmasters, who were known for including the gulls in the population of Marrow Bay.

**fennick** — Something that works but produces nothing, after Fennick's Mill on the Quill, whose wheel still turns but has ground nothing in living memory. Calling a committee "fennick" is a mild insult.

**the Strand ledger** — The record of passing ships kept at Hollin Lighthouse on the Grey Isles since the time of the keeper Agathe Strand. Asking to see it is polite; asking to copy it is not.

**the Northern Trade League** — The trading union of northern sea states that Zeltavia joined in 1987, much later than its neighbours, after a council debate that lasted, by tradition, eleven lantern nights.
